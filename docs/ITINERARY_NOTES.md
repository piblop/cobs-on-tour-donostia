# Itinerary inspiration

Design notes drawn from the crew's real trip plan, used as inspiration for content and pacing.
The spec (`docs/GAME_SPEC.md`) still decides structure. Per the spec's privacy rules, this file keeps
first names only and carries no dates, prices, addresses or booking details.

## The crew's own voice

- The plan is "advice transcribed faithfully from Annette", with tips from a friend, Stuart.
  Morning planning can present suggestions as **Annette's tips** and **Stuart says** notes.
- Annette's commandments, perfect for banter and wishes:
  - "As many tomato salads as possible while in Basque country."
  - La Viña's burnt cheesecake is "the best cheesecake on the planet. Non-negotiable." (She wants it twice.)
  - "Coast road, not the highway."
  - "Pack for everything": gumboots and a raincoat for rain and chilly nights.

## Donostia, as the crew plans it

- **Monte Igueldo first.** A "dodgy, hectic old amusement park with the best view of the bay",
  reached by the creaky funicular. A strong first-morning suggestion.
- **Old town:** worth exploring, but "more tourists than locals". Good for a gently grumpy banter line.
- **Morning txakoli** and the **Bretxa fish market**.
- **Peine del Viento** at the west end of the bay, **Monte Urgull** with the old fort and statue,
  and the full **La Concha** crescent walk, "railings and all".
- **Remote mountain sagardotegi (cider house):** "ask locals, drive to the most remote one".

## Food set pieces

**The pintxos crawl** (fits Pintxos Crawl + Tortilla Rush):
- Bar Néstor: put your name down early for the limited tortilla; tomato salad fulfils the commandment;
  txuleta to share.
- Gandarias: the clam mission, almejas a la marinera ("bread for the sauce is not optional"), and a solomillo pintxo.
- La Cuchara de San Telmo: veal cheek in red wine.
- La Viña: burnt Basque cheesecake to finish.
- One pintxo and one txakoli per bar.

**The cider-house feast** (fits Txotx!): a fixed menu that simply arrives:
chorizo cooked in cider → salt-cod omelette → fried cod with green peppers → txuleta off the coals →
Idiazabal cheese with quince paste and walnuts → cider from the barrel whenever someone shouts "txotx!".

**Tasting-menu option:** Arzak, the father-and-daughter house of New Basque Cuisine
(star count stays `needsReview: true` per the spec).

**Coast-road day trip** (fits Getaria): Orio's riverside asador with whole wild fish over coals
(besugo), the Zarautz beach stroll, the Getaria–Zarautz clifftop path, and Getaria's famous
grilled turbot. Asador rules: sharp green salad, let them portion the fish, chase the cheek meat.
